# Results — 2026-08-07 inference power

Per-inference power and energy for all four TFLite Micro workloads, captured on
the S4AI testbench with the
[power-measurement-pipeline](https://github.com/S4AI-CornellTech/power-measurement-pipeline).

Column names match the `## Inference Power` section of [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md),
so these tables can be pasted in unchanged. That includes its existing
`avg_infernce_duration_s` misspelling, kept deliberately rather than silently
diverging.

This is a separate file rather than an edit to `RESULTS.md` because that file's
numbers come from an earlier campaign, on a rig that still had the STM32F411VE.
Merging the two is a decision about which run is canonical, not a formatting
change.

## Method

The pipeline's `main.py` runs one (device, workload) pair at a time and enforces
the power sequencing in `run_measurement()`: the DUT is never fed from two
sources at once, and its USB hub port is cut so USB cannot back-feed the
measured rail. Wiring is documented in
[switchboard/HARDWARE.md](switchboard/HARDWARE.md).

- **Supply**: Nordic PPK2 in source mode at **3300 mV**, feeding the board's
  3V3 pin through the relay switch board.
- **Sampling**: 100 kHz. Raw per-sample CSVs are kept for every row.
- **Inference boundaries**: the firmware drives a GPIO marker HIGH for the
  duration of `Invoke()` only. That pin is routed through the analog mux into
  the PPK2 logic port (D0). The pipeline's `power_test.analyze()` counts only
  pulses it has seen *both* edges of, so a partially captured inference is
  never counted.
- **Guard**: a capture with zero complete pulses, or with implausibly low
  current, is refused rather than written. Raw samples are still kept for
  diagnosis.

### Column meanings

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `active_time_s` | total time the marker was HIGH, i.e. summed inference time |
| `avg_current_mA` | mean current over the **whole capture window**, not just inference |
| `peak_current_mA` | maximum single sample in the window |
| `avg_power_mW` | `avg_current_mA` x 3.3 V |
| `total_energy_mJ` | energy over the **whole capture window** |
| `inferences_in_sample` | complete marker pulses counted |
| `avg_infernce_duration_s` | `active_time_s / inferences_in_sample` |
| `avg_inf_energy_mJ` | mean energy of one inference, integrated over its pulse |

### Capture windows

The window must hold several complete inferences, so it is sized per device and
workload in the pipeline's `config.MEASURE_TIME_S*`. All rows below used
**9.994 s** except:

| board | workload | window |
|---|---|---|
| nrf52840 | VWW | 109.995 s |
| nrf52840 | PersonDetection | 199.996 s |

The nRF52840 is a 64 MHz Cortex-M4 with no NN accelerator; a single VWW
inference takes ~10.7 s, so a 10 s window did not contain one complete pulse.

### CPU clocks

Every row is at each chip's **160 MHz** default. This matters for esp32S3
KWSLarge specifically — see the caveats below.

## Caveats

**`total_energy_mJ` is not comparable across rows.** It covers the whole capture
window, and those windows differ — 9.994 s for most rows, 109.995 s and
199.996 s for the two nrf52840 rows. The nrf52840 PersonDetection figure of
4648 mJ is not "more energy than esp32's 1620 mJ"; it is a 20x longer window.
Use `avg_power_mW` and `avg_inf_energy_mJ` for comparisons.

**esp32S3 PersonDetection is missing.** Not yet captured. Every other cell in
the grid is populated.

**esp32S3 KWSLarge is the 160 MHz run, deliberately.** `micro_speech_large` was
the only workload whose `sdkconfig.defaults` used modern IDF-5 option names, so
it was the only one that actually received the 240 MHz it asked for; the other
three ask via `CONFIG_ESP32_DEFAULT_CPU_FREQ_MHZ`, a symbol that no longer
exists, and silently build at the 160 MHz default. That made it the only
workload clocked differently from the rest, and on the PPK2-fed 3V3 rail it
intermittently brownout-reset before reaching the inference loop. For reference,
the same workload at 240 MHz measured 0.145 s and 49.240 mJ per inference —
faster *and* lower energy, since the shorter runtime outweighs the higher power.
Reporting 240 MHz would require re-measuring esp32 and esp32S3 across all four
workloads.

**nrf52840 KWSLarge rests on 2 inferences.** Its 20 s window against a 4.03 s
inference leaves a thin sample; every other cell has 10 or more. A 60 s window
would give ~14.

**stmf411ve is absent.** The STM32F411VE is no longer on the rig, having been
swapped for the nRF52840. Its rows in [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md) predate this run.

**rp2040 per-inference energy tracks the idle baseline.** Its active and idle
current differ by well under 1 mA, so `avg_inf_energy_mJ` is very nearly
`avg_power_mW x avg_infernce_duration_s`. That is a property of the board, not a
measurement artifact.
